* **A_base** — original setup + weight-decay fix
* **B_relay_rocof** — A + RoCoF reward from the 500 ms relay window (w 25, hinge 20)
* **C_smoothing** — A + actuator low-pass (0.08 s) + action-rate penalty
* **D_obs_norm** — A + running observation normalisation
* **E_reward_scale** — A + learner reward scaled to [-12, 0] (Adam eps 1e-10)
* **F_rocof_stress** — A + RoCoF-stress training episodes
* **ALL** — every change (the results_measured_v2 setup)
