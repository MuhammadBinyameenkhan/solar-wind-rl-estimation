What the thesis is expected to deliver. I state each outcome as a hypothesis with the test that decides it, so a negative result is still an answer. H1: retraining with joint scaling should lower converter saturation compared with clipping, without losing return, tested by a paired Wilcoxon test. H2: TD3 should at least match DDPG in all three microgrids. H3 is the central one: the oracle bound tells us where learning can pay off; where it exceeds about five percent, in weakly coupled plants, TD3 should beat a tuned VSG, and where it does not, the conclusion is that a tuned VSG with the feasibility layer is enough. H4: rankings should not depend on the reward weights. H5: the averaged-model conclusions should hold at circuit level over at least 40 scenarios and all seeds. No final numbers are promised; the right column is today's evidence. Outcomes: a validated feasibility layer and a decision rule academically; a deployable architecture with a safe fallback, RoCoF-window guidance and open code practically; and three papers plus a fourth from the weak-coupling study.
