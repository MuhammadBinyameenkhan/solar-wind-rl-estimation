A VSG makes the battery converter emulate the swing equation of a synchronous machine. Because J and D are just numbers in the controller, they can be changed online, by rules or by learning. But they are not free: the inertial term commits power proportional to RoCoF and the damping term commits power proportional to the deviation. At the grid-code limits of Study 1, 2 Hz/s and 0.5 Hz on a 50 Hz base, one second of inertia commits the same headroom as eight per-unit of damping.
