B22

Several results point the same way. Under the Study 1 limits one second of inertia costs as much headroom as eight per-unit of damping. The tuned fixed VSG in Study 1 chose zero inertia and 35 per-unit damping. The learned policies converge to about 0.4 seconds of inertia and 21 to 23 per-unit damping, raising damping to 35 to 50 at each contingency and dropping it once the deviation is arrested. In Study 2 the oracle's damping hardly changes with headroom. So headroom-limited VSG control is mainly a damping-scheduling problem. Source: Sections 4.2.2, 4.2.3, 4.3.2.
