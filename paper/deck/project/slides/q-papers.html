The papers do not overlap. TREC introduced the headroom requirement and derating in a single-seed study. IREMOS repeats Study 1 with ten seeds and tuned baselines and shows that learning trades regulation for headroom. IREA moves to the hybrid diesel plant with Thai data, adds the oracle bound, compares feasibility rules and validates at circuit level.
