This map places each study by two questions: how do the parameters adapt, and what limits them. The bottom row is the crowded one: most adaptive and learning schemes do not check converter capability at all. In the middle row, limits exist but are fixed at design time: a static inertia bound from the rated power in Shi, state-of-charge bands in Han, a fixed inertia–damping region in Lu and Zhuan, and fixed power bounds in a safety filter in Egbomwan 2025. The top row, headroom that follows the energy-management dispatch, is empty except for this thesis. Positions are qualitative and based on each paper's stated method.
