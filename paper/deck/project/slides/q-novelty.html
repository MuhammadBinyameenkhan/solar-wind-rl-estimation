B5

The closest works do put limits on J and D, but all are fixed: a static bound from the rated power in Shi, a fixed J–D region in Lu and Zhuan, fixed power bounds in Egbomwan's safety filter, an SoC band in Han. None of them changes with the power the converter has already committed to the energy-management dispatch. That is the quantity my constraint tracks, separately upward and downward, every 50 ms. The other two contributions are the evaluation protocol, with an oracle bound, tuned baselines and multi-seed statistics, which none of the eleven external studies used, and circuit-level validation.
