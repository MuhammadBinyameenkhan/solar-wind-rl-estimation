The first refinement in Study 2 is direction. Headroom is what is left between the EMS dispatch and the battery's limits. In this illustration the battery is rated plus or minus 0.5 megawatts and is already discharging 0.3 for the evening peak. It has only 0.2 megawatts upward, plus the PV reserve, but 0.8 downward. An under-frequency event uses the upward headroom, an over-frequency event the downward. The limits taper with state of charge. This is why the feasible inertia changes hour by hour over the year.
