Study 1, ten seeds, converter-only microgrid. Literature-style high settings are simply not deliverable: fifteen seconds and sixty per-unit would need 1.8 per-unit of headroom, 4500 kVA on a 2500 kVA converter, and they are derated in every step. On the five deterministic test windows the tuned constant VSG regulates best, 1.73 against 1.84 hertz-seconds for TD3, but it reserves the most headroom and is derated in 41 percent of steps. On the fifty held-out stress episodes the advantage disappears: TD3 reaches 2.15 against 2.18, and both learned controllers reserve 19 to 21 percent less headroom on every seed.
