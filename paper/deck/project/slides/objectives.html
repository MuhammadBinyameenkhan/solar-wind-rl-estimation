1 · Objectives and scope

Four objectives, two studies

O1

Formulate a converter-headroom feasibility constraint on J and D, and compare its alternative forms.

O2

Develop DDPG- and TD3-based adaptive VSGs under the constraint, for two microgrids driven by real weather data.

O3

Evaluate fairly against tuned constant and rule-based VSGs, over several seeds with statistics, and bound the achievable gain.

O4

Validate beyond the averaged model: independent MATLAB implementation and three-phase Simscape EMT model.

Scope

Study 1 · 2.5 MVA converter-only microgrid, measured 1-Hz weather, 10 seeds

Study 2 · 2 MVA diesel–PV–wind–BESS microgrid, 2024 ERA5 + NASA POWER data for Nakhon Ratchasima, 5 seeds

Out of scope: multi-bus networks, protection, switching-level converter models, hardware tests (planned if available)

7

Four objectives, one per research question. The work is organised as two connected studies. Study 1 develops the headroom-feasible learning framework in a fully converter-interfaced microgrid with measured one-hertz weather data. Study 2 transfers it to a hybrid microgrid with a diesel generator, driven by one year of site data for Nakhon Ratchasima, refines the constraint and adds circuit-level validation. I am explicit about scope: single bus, no protection modelling and no switching-level converter model.
