Three numbers frame the problem. A converter can carry only twenty to forty percent overcurrent, while a synchronous machine can carry several times its rating. Alipoor and colleagues showed in the lab that a VSG with fixed inertia tripped on overcurrent after a modest power step. And in my Thai test case about 44 percent of the energy is renewable, so the frequency increasingly depends on how the battery converter is controlled.
