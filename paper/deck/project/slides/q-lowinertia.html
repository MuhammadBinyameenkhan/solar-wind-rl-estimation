B1

Without fast support the systems are not operable. In Study 1, without a VSG, the nadir falls from 49.09 Hz on the clear day to 47.68 Hz in combined stress, and the 500 ms RoCoF reaches 1.83 to 4.58 Hz/s, above the 2 Hz/s relay limit in four of five scenarios. In Study 2 the diesel alone collapses in 97.5 percent of the test scenarios; BESS droop without inertia keeps it stable but at 5.5 Hz/s RoCoF; the tuned VSG brings RoCoF down to about 1.5 Hz/s. Source: proposal Section 4.2.1, Table 4.4. The 45–90 percent figure is the 0.2–0.4 MW step range divided by the 0.45 MW mean load of Table 4.2.
