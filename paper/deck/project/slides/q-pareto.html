B21

Both, depending on the axis, which is why the weight-free Pareto view matters. With the measured load TD3 improves the nadir by 0.044 Hz but spends 0.024 kWh more battery energy per episode, so it sits at a different point of the trade-off the fixed settings already span. It is not dominated, but only narrowly: the fixed setting of 8 seconds and 15 per-unit reaches the same nadir, 0.408 against 0.407 Hz, with less fast energy, less time outside the band and lower RoCoF. The honest conclusion is that learning moves the controller along the trade-off rather than beyond it in this plant. Source: Section 4.3.4, Fig. 4.9.
