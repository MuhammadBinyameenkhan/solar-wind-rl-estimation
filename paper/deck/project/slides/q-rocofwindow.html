Q: Why RoCoFlim = 1 Hz/s, and over which window?

What 1 Hz/s is

A sizing value for the inertial commitment in Study 2, not a bound the controllers are guaranteed to meet. Study 1 used the 2 Hz/s relay limit of the earlier work.

Window matters

Interpreted over 500 ms, as used for RoCoF protection. The reported metric uses 100 ms, which is stricter.

Source

Stated as a design choice; the grid-code or utility reference (PEA/EGAT) is being added.

B12

The committee may notice that the 100 ms RoCoF in Table 4.4 is above 1 Hz/s for every VSG controller. That is consistent: 1 Hz/s is a sizing value for the inertial commitment, interpreted over a 500 ms window as used for RoCoF protection; over 500 ms the twelve validation scenarios stay at 0.90 to 0.96 Hz/s. In the EMT model the 100 ms RoCoF is about 2.5 times higher because the PLL reads the 3 degree voltage phase jump at each load step as a frequency spike, while the 500 ms value changes by only minus 0.5 to plus 9 percent.
