What can be said now

The direction of the TD3 effect survives at circuit level; its size does not. No significance test is attempted on 12 scenarios. 0.038 Hz is this 12-scenario subset; 0.061 Hz is the 200-scenario, 5-seed mean.

Candidate causes (to be tested)

The policy observes PLL-measured frequency and RoCoF, which spike in EMT, so it sees inputs it never saw in training; and the limiter clips its extra fast power more often.

The honest answer: the direction is real, the size is not established. On the twelve EMT scenarios, using one policy, TD3 seed 1 final, the nadir advantage over the tuned VSG falls from 0.038 Hz in the averaged model to 0.019 Hz. Note that 0.038 refers to these twelve scenarios and this one seed, whereas 0.061 Hz is the 200-scenario, five-seed mean. Two plausible causes, which I have not yet tested: the policy's inputs come from the PLL and see the phase-jump spikes, a distribution shift from training; and the limiter cuts the extra fast power that gives TD3 its nadir advantage. The extended EMT study with at least 40 scenarios and all seeds will test whether the advantage remains significant. Source: Section 4.3.5, Table 1.2 H5.
