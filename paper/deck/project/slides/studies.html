The thesis has two connected studies. Study 1, published as TREC and IREMOS, builds the headroom-feasible learning framework in a converter-only microgrid with residual inertia of half a second and measured one-hertz weather data, over ten seeds. Study 2 moves to a hybrid microgrid where a two-second diesel generator shares every step, uses a full year of site data for Nakhon Ratchasima, refines the feasibility rule to be direction-aware and time-separated, and adds MATLAB and EMT validation. Study 2 changes what matters physically: a real synchronous machine, real site data and a stricter definition of feasibility. [Update the TREC and IREMOS status if it changes before the exam.]
