B14

The Markov decision process of Study 2. The observation has 16 normalised entries, including both directional headrooms, so the agent can see when its commitments will be cut. The action is residual: zero reproduces the validation-tuned VSG, positive moves a parameter towards its feasible upper bound, negative towards its minimum. Every command passes the 0.25 second rate limiter and then the feasibility layer. The cost penalises the frequency outside the band, RoCoF above 1 Hz/s, fast VSG power, BESS power, saturation and violations, with weights 1, 0.02, 0.5, 2, 0.1, 2 and 1. In the code there are also two small action terms, 0.05 on the residual size and 0.3 on its rate, which keep the policy near the tuned VSG and avoid bang-bang switching. [AUTHOR: these two action terms are in configs/default.yaml but not yet in Eq. 3.23 of the proposal.] A discount of 0.99 at 50 ms steps gives a horizon of about 5 seconds.
