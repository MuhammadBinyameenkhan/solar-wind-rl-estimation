The second refinement is timing. The summed rule of my earlier studies assumes the worst RoCoF and the worst deviation happen together. In a swing response they do not: RoCoF peaks immediately after the step, when the deviation is still near zero, and the deviation peaks at the nadir, where RoCoF is zero. So each mechanism is sized at its own peak: the larger of the two commitments must fit the headroom. Note that the 1 hertz-per-second limit is a sizing value over a 500 millisecond window; over 100 milliseconds every controller exceeds it, which I will come back to.
