The review follows PRISMA 2020. Four databases, 2015 to 2026, two concept blocks: the VSG or virtual-inertia block and the adaptation or learning block. At full-text screening I excluded studies that do not adapt the parameters, that report no quantitative frequency results, that are not peer reviewed, or that duplicate the same model. Seventeen studies were included. The eleven adaptive-VSG studies among them were read in full, and every number on the next slides is quoted from their tables and figures.
