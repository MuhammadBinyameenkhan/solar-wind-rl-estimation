Fifteen years of progress made the parameters smarter; almost none made them deliverable.

M. B. Khan · Feasible synthetic inertia · Proposal defence

9

The field moved in clear steps. In 2011 the synchronverter put the machine equations into the controller with fixed parameters. From 2015 rules began to adapt inertia, then inertia and damping together. In parallel, current-limiting studies showed that the converter rating bounds what grid-forming control can deliver. From 2023 deep reinforcement learning entered: DDPG, TD3 and SAC, with one study on a real-time digital simulator, and recent work benchmarks the algorithms against each other. The common thread: the parameters became smarter, but almost no study checked that they can be delivered. That is where this thesis starts.
