Table 3.1 of the proposal, plus the measured cost. Each Study 2 run is about 600,000 environment steps and took roughly one hour on CPU: TD3 52 to 54 minutes, DDPG 48 to 68, TD3 without the constraint 68 to 72 minutes, so the 15 Study 2 runs are about 15 CPU-hours. Inference is cheap: the Study 2 actor, 16 inputs, two hidden layers of 256 and 3 outputs, has 70,915 parameters, about 71,000 multiply-adds per decision every 50 ms, which a modern DSP or embedded controller handles easily.
