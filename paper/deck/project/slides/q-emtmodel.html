B26

The EMT model, built with Simscape Electrical Specialized Power Systems, is a three-phase 400 volt system on the 2 MVA base. The VSG is an average-model converter behind its coupling impedance; the diesel is an internal voltage behind its reactance with swing equation, governor and AGC; the loads and renewable base power are a PLL-synchronised grid-following injection. The coupling reactances are set to one over the synchronising coefficients, 0.5 and 1.25 per-unit, so the linearised behaviour matches the averaged model by construction, and any difference is due to physics the averaged model leaves out. The step is 100 microseconds. An independent script solver of the same circuit agrees with Simscape within about 2 percent. Source: Section 3.10; matlab/simscape/vsgrl_emt_params.m.
