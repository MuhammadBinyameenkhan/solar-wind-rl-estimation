B6

Because the reference is different. The published gains are measured against default fixed parameters or PI and PID control, each from a single trained agent on one to three disturbances; only Li et al. 2026 tuned a baseline. My reference is a VSG tuned on validation data with the same objective, and the gain is measured over 200 test scenarios and five seeds with a corrected significance test. Against that reference the nadir gain is 10 percent, and the oracle bound shows nothing could do better than 1.3 percent on the objective in this plant. This is the point of the comparison: the reported gain depends heavily on what it is compared with. Source: Section 4.4, Table 4.6.
