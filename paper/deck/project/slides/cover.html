Thesis proposal examination

Toward Feasible Synthetic Inertia

Reinforcement-learning-driven virtual synchronous generator control for low-inertia PV–wind–BESS microgrids

Candidate

Muhammad Binyameen Khan

Advisor

Assoc. Prof. Dr. Anant Oonsivilai

School of Electrical Engineering · Institute of Engineering · Suranaree University of Technology · [Date of examination]

Good morning. My thesis asks a practical question: when a battery converter is told to behave like a synchronous generator, can it actually deliver the inertia and damping it promises, and does learning-based scheduling of those parameters really help once it is compared fairly? I will cover the motivation, a systematic literature review, the methodology, the preliminary results of two completed studies, and the plan to completion.
