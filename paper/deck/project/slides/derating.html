Third, when the requirement exceeds the headroom, the command must be brought back. Under the time-separated rule the feasible set is a rectangle in the J–D plane. Clipping moves only the violating parameter to its bound and keeps the other. Proportional derating, the method of Study 1, scales both by one factor along the ray to the origin and keeps their ratio. Both are compared in the results. The RL agents in Study 2 were trained with clipping; the comparison shows scaling is better, so retraining with it is part of the remaining work.
