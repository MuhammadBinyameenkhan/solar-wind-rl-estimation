Safety is layered and does not rely on the network behaving well. The residual action is bounded, a rate limiter smooths parameter changes, the headroom projection makes every commitment deliverable, and below that the converter's own limiter and protection remain. The projection cut undeliverable commitments of the learned policy from 1.99 to 0.01 seconds per 30 second episode. What I cannot claim is that the converter never saturates: the saturation time even rose slightly, and the retraining with joint scaling is meant to address it. Source: Sections 3.6, 4.3.4.
