M. B. Khan · Feasible synthetic inertia · Proposal defence

12

This matrix turns the review into gaps, each checked against the full texts. One: no study checks the commanded inertia and damping against dispatch-dependent headroom. Two: tuned baselines are rare. Three: none of the eleven reports results over several training seeds with significance tests. Four: only four model a synchronous machine. Five: three go beyond software simulation, with a lab inverter, hardware-in-the-loop or a real-time simulator. My work closes the first four. On the fifth, I am honest: my validation is an EMT model, not hardware, and Han and Oboreh-Snapps go further there. HIL is future work.
