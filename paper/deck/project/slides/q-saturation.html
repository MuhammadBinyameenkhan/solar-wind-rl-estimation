Why it can happen

The constraint sizes the commitment at the RoCoF and band limits. Saturation is set by the actual excursion, which also contains secondary-control and EMS set-point movement.

With a synthetic load (earlier)

Saturation rose slightly with clipping (0.103 → 0.123 s). With measured demand, more headroom remains and the effect disappears.

Remedy being tested (H1)

Retrain with joint scaling, which keeps J/D in ratio and lowered saturation where the constraint binds.

B11

The constraint guarantees that the commitment is deliverable at the design limits; it does not guarantee that the converter never reaches its limit, because the real power excursion also includes secondary-control and EMS set-point movement. With the measured EGAT load the converter is at its limit for only about 0.013 seconds per episode, and the constraint does not change that significantly: 0.0131 against 0.0135 seconds for the tuned VSG, p 0.85, and 0.0162 against 0.0185 for TD3, p 0.64. With the earlier synthetic load, which left less headroom, clipping raised saturation slightly; that effect disappears with real demand. Joint scaling is never worse than clipping and is significantly better for the high and largest settings, so the agents will be retrained with scaling; that is hypothesis H1.
