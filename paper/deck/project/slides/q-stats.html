Study 2 uses the paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test, because each scenario is run by every controller, so differences are paired, and they are not normally distributed; Holm controls the family-wise error across all comparisons. With the measured EGAT load, TD3 beats the tuned VSG on the nadir in 88.5 percent of scenarios, p Holm 8 times 10 to the minus 25, and now also slightly on RoCoF, p 0.04; it settles later, uses more BESS energy, and the tuned VSG has the better objective. Against DDPG, TD3 is better on nadir and RoCoF with p below 10 to the minus 17. Study 1 uses means with 95 percent intervals over ten seeds. Source: results/significance.csv, Section 3.9.
