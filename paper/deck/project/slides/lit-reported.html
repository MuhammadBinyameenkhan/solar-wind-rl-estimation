Every value here is quoted from the papers' own tables or figures. The published improvements are large, 33 to 60 percent, but each is measured against a fixed-parameter VSG or a PI controller with default settings, from one trained agent. In my Study 2, against a VSG tuned on validation data, over five seeds and 200 test scenarios, TD3 reduces the nadir by 10 percent, and the oracle shows no scheduler could improve the objective by more than 1.3 percent. The point is not that my agents are weaker. It is how much a reported gain depends on the reference it is measured against. Sources: Shi Sec. 5.2; Egbomwan 2023 Sec. V; Han Table 3; Lu and Zhuan Table 2; Li 2017 Fig. 2; Criollo abstract; Li 2026 Table 3.
