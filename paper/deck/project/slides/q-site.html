B4

The site of the thesis is Nakhon Ratchasima. With one year of data a chronological split would put a whole season in the test set, so the hours are divided into weekly blocks, five for training, one for validation and one for testing in every seven, which keeps all seasons in every subset. ERA5 reanalysis gives the 100 m wind, interpolated bilinearly from a 2 by 2 grid; NASA POWER gives irradiance and temperature, converted from local solar time to UTC. Air density comes from temperature and pressure at 226 m. Inside each hour, 10 percent turbulence and 0.5 percent load noise are added, and two or three load steps of 0.2 to 0.4 MW.
