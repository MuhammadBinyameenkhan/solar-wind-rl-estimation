26

This is the main positive result. Trained without the constraint, TD3 learns to commit more than the converter can deliver: 0.9 to 5.2 seconds per episode depending on the seed, mean 1.99, almost four times the tuned VSG. With the constraint this falls to 0.01 seconds. For the tuned VSG, removing infeasible commitments costs almost nothing. Two caveats, reported openly. First, the constraint does not change the time the converter actually sits at its power limit, which is short with the measured load; it guarantees the commitment, not the current limit. Second, the unconstrained TD3 reaches a better nadir, 0.386 against 0.407 hertz, but part of that gain comes from commitments the converter could not have delivered; that is exactly what the constraint is meant to prevent.
