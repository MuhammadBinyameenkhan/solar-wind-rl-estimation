Q: Why size by the max, and not by the sum?

Physics

RoCoF peaks just after the step, when Δf ≈ 0. Δf peaks at the nadir, when RoCoF = 0. The two powers do not occur together, so each is sized at its own peak.

B10

The summed rule of Study 1 assumes the worst RoCoF and the worst deviation at the same instant, which does not happen: RoCoF is largest right after the step, when the deviation is still near zero, and the deviation is largest at the nadir, where RoCoF is zero. The time-separated rule sizes each at its own peak. In Table 4.3 the summed rule with the same limits saves a little more saturation and energy but deepens the nadir; with the wider Study 1 limits it collapses every setting to about 2 seconds and 11 to 14 per-unit, and the nadir of the tuned setting degrades from 0.45 to 0.62 Hz. So the summed rule is too strict in a system with physical inertia. Source: Section 4.3.3, Table 4.3.
