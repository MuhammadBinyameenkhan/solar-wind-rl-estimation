B9

In Study 2 each mechanism is sized at its own limit. The inertial power at the RoCoF limit is 2 J_v times 0.02 per-unit per second, so J_v can be at most 25 times the headroom; the damping power at the band edge is D_v times 0.004, so D_v can be at most 250 times the headroom, all on the 2 MVA base. With an idle battery and 0.5 MW of headroom the bounds are loose, 6.25 seconds and the 50 per-unit cap; in the evening peak with only 0.1 MW left, the tuned setting of 3 seconds and 15 per-unit is not deliverable and is cut to 1.25 seconds and 12.5 per-unit. These are worked examples of the equation, not simulation outputs. Code: vsgrl/envs/vsg_env.py, param_bounds.
