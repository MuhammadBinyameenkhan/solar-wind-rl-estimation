This is the key number for research question three, and I had it before training any agent. With the measured EGAT load, on forty validation scenarios the best single fixed setting reaches a return of minus 94.71; an oracle that picks the best of 24 settings separately for each scenario reaches minus 93.48. So any scenario-level adaptive scheduler can improve the objective by at most 1.3 percent. The reasons are physical: the synchronous coupling gives the VSG about 71 percent of each step instantly, secondary control restores frequency within 10 to 20 seconds, and the best damping, about 12 per-unit, hardly depends on headroom. This is why I propose the oracle as a go/no-go test before training.
