B32

Generality is addressed in three ways. The two existing plants already differ in the way that matters, converter-only versus a synchronous machine sharing the transient, and the feasibility result holds in both while the learning benefit does not; that contrast is itself a finding. The weak-coupling study varies exactly the factors the oracle says control the benefit. And the oracle bound is a procedure, not a number, so a designer can apply it to a new plant before training.
