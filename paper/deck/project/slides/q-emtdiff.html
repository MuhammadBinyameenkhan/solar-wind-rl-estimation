B27

Over twelve scenarios and three controllers the EMT frequency follows the averaged model within 13 to 17 millihertz RMS, the nadir within 2 percent, BESS energy within 1.1 percent. Two differences are physical. At each load step the bus voltage phase jumps by about 3 degrees; the PLL reads that as a brief frequency spike, so the 100 ms RoCoF is about 2.5 times higher, while the 500 ms RoCoF hardly changes. And the converter limiter, acting on measured power including those transients, is active 2.2 to 2.8 times longer. The ordering of controllers is the same except for RoCoF, where controllers differ by at most 6 percent, so I claim no RoCoF ranking. Source: Section 4.3.5, Table 4.5, Fig. 4.10.
