The diesel generator and the VSG are voltage sources behind reactances meeting at the load bus. Linearised, the network shares any imbalance in proportion to their synchronising coefficients, and the bus frequency is their weighted mean speed. Each machine then follows its own swing equation; the VSG's virtual inertia and damping are the parameters the agent schedules. The VSG power is limited to what the battery has left after the EMS dispatch plus the PV share. One consequence matters for everything later: with K_d of 0.8 and K_s of 2.0, about 71 percent of every load step lands on the VSG instantly, regardless of J_v and D_v.
